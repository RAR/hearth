What's pinned by the spec

  • Dot speed is data: a lap takes 4 s at 50 W, scaling linearly down to 1.2 s at 4 kW+. Two dots per line, half a lap apart.
  • Dim lines are structure: inactive connections stay drawn at 12% white so the diamond never changes shape — flows under 50 W simply don't animate.
  • Dot color = source (solar amber, grid gray, battery green), so direction and origin read together.
  • The ring keeps the gauge's language: green charging, amber discharging, holding its last color while idle. Sweep = state of charge.
  • One renderer, two sizes: everything scales from the box, so card and panel cannot drift apart. The Show 5 keeps its compact pill and never sees any of this.
